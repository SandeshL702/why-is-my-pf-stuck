# Why is my PF stuck?
A zero-login EPFO claim diagnostic. Paste rejection text or pick a symptom for a plain-English diagnosis, checklist, HR email and EPFiGMS grievance draft.

Live: https://sandeshl702.github.io/why-is-my-pf-stuck/

Covers: date of exit, name/DOB mismatch, KYC, bank/IFSC, employer, wrong form/EPS, transfer, and unclear status.

Runs in the browser. No login, uploads, UAN collection, analytics, or personal data. Not legal or financial advice.
